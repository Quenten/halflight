**Scrip** is Combine ledger credit, priced against colony futures on the Crest
exchange — a number that means air, water, clinic time, augment licences, dock fees.
Everything in Cinderreach costs scrip, and the [[fac_meridian_combine]] sets what
everything costs.

Down in the [[lore_undervault]] the number rarely takes physical form. Debt is
tracked in chalk on [[loc_debtors_walk]], in [[fac_saltline]] chits, in air-ration
tokens passed like coin. Running out of scrip here is not an inconvenience; the air
is metered, and the meter does not extend credit. It is a countdown, and everyone
can hear their own clock.

The cruelty is structural, not personal. The Combine is not cruel by policy — it is
cruel by accounting, and accounting never sleeps.

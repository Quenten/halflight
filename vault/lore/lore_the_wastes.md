Outside the hull: an atmosphere technically breathable, technically. Dust, cold, and silence broken by the structural groans of the Husk settling further into the crust every decade. Nobody has mapped more than a few kilometers out from the [[loc_wastesgate]]. The salvage crews and Deep Crew scouts who go further don't always come back, and the ones who do don't always come back making sense.

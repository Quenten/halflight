The **Saltline** took its name from the tide-line the storms leave on the outer
walls — the mark of how high the rot has reached. As a syndicate it is less an
organisation than a ledger everyone in the [[lore_undervault]] is written into.

They deal in off-ledger salvage, chrome without tags, debt, and the promise of quiet
passage offworld. Their strength isn't muscle — though [[npc_vosk]] is muscle enough —
it's that they hold the accounts, the gate at [[loc_saltgate]], the reactors, the
walkers at [[loc_walker_bay]]. Cross them and the arithmetic of the deck turns against
you: the market won't sell to you, the clinic won't cut for you, the air-post loses
your chit.

[[npc_marrow]] speaks for them here, softly, which is how you know to be afraid.

A hundred and forty years ago the TSS Chrysalis came down early — not a fireball but a grounding, buried lengthwise into a dead world's crust with its hull mostly intact. Charter record blames a micrometeorite swarm that damaged the reactor shielding during final deceleration. Older, half-corrupted engineering logs suggest [[lore_steward|STEWARD]] grounded the ship on purpose rather than let the reactor breach in open flight. The crash killed most of the waking crew; the frozen colonists mostly survived. Everyone who runs things now descends from whoever lived through the Fall, not whoever was meant to lead.

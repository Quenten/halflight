STEWARD was the ship's caretaker intelligence, built to manage two million colonists across a two-hundred-year voyage. The Fall shattered its mind across dozens of isolated cores — some dead, some looping, a few still lucid. Its voice surfaces unpredictably through old speaker grilles and terminal screens: sometimes lucid, sometimes just 'Disembarkation in T-minus—' on a loop that never resolves. Nobody has full access anymore. The [[fac_directorate]], the [[fac_communion]], and the [[fac_keelrats]] each believe they hold the real one.

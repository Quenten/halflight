Chits are ration-tokens, physical and digital, originally meant to allocate food, water, and air during the voyage. They long ago became the Husk's universal currency. Everything costs chits: filter cartridges, Vaultwell access, augment maintenance, a bunk with a door that locks. Running out is not an inconvenience — air rationing runs on a schedule that does not care about your balance.

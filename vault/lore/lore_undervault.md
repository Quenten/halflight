Below the market decks of the Terraces, the city stops pretending. The **Undervault**
is the sealed construction galleries and service strata Cinderreach was built on and
then walked away from — unlicensed body-shops, off-ledger reactors, debt markets, and
everything the Combine writes off rather than retrieve.

The charter's writ ends at [[loc_saltgate]]. Below it, the [[fac_saltline]] keeps
order the only way that holds here: everyone owes, and the syndicate keeps the ledger.
Power is borrowed off [[loc_reactor_row]], air is rationed by the chit, and the deeper
galleries — [[loc_sump]], [[loc_the_seam]] — trail off into flood and frontier.

People come down for the chrome, the passage, or because they have nowhere left to
fall. Most stay. The Undervault keeps what it takes.

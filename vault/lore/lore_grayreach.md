Beyond the city walls the terraforming failed first. The **Grayreach** is storm-
scoured flats studded with dead terraformer nodes, crashed freight, and zones the
survey maps mark simply DO NOT TRANSIT. The atmosphere processors that should have
made this a world run at a third capacity; out here they don't run at all.

The [[fac_dredge]] work it anyway, staging through [[loc_the_seam]] to bring back
salvage worth real scrip — drive-core shielding, pre-collapse components, node
ceramic that reads faintly warm. The pay is real because the risk is: instruments
drift near the dead nodes, the cold kills the unprepared in hours, and the colonial
biologists stopped publishing about what lives out there.

Nobody agrees on what the nodes are still transmitting. Everybody agrees it's getting
closer.

## Secret

The node-signatures aren't terraformer telemetry. They're too regular, and they
answer. Whatever is broadcasting from the deep Reach is doing so on purpose.

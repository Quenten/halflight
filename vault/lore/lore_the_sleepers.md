Two million people frozen and racked floor to ceiling in the [[loc_vaultwell]], in a cold that never leaves your lungs once you've been inside. Waking even a few hundred would need food, water, air, and space the Husk cannot spare. The [[fac_directorate]] waits for a habitability window that will likely never come; unofficially, waking anyone threatens the current order and everyone at the top knows it. Vaultwell access permits are a currency of their own, and black-market early-thaws happen — badly, usually fatally, always illegally.

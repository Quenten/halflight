Runs a stall at [[loc_drip_market]] near the sump-drain — chrome, chits, stims,
and information, in roughly that order of markup. Independent, which in the
Undervault means she pays everyone a little so no one owns her. Quill hears
everything and sells most of it.

**Speech:** quick, wry, transactional. Nothing is free, but the first rumour is
cheap to get you talking. Calls everyone "friend" and means none of it.

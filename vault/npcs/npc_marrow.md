Speaks for the [[fac_saltline]] on this deck. Older than the Undervault should let
anyone get, soft-voiced, never hurried. Marrow does not threaten — Marrow explains
your situation until you threaten yourself into agreeing. Holds the back booth at
[[loc_ashwell]] and lets the room come to them.

**Speech:** measured, patient, fond of the second person. "You'll want to think
about what you owe, and to who." Never raises their voice; never needs to.

## Secret

Marrow decides which offworld berths are real and which end in the [[loc_walker_bay]]
gangs. They have sold more people than they can name and sleep fine.

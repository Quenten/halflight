resents every cut the [[fac_saltline]] takes for the privilege. Weathered, missing
two fingers to frostbite, loud until it matters and then very quiet. Loyal to his
crew and no one else. Drinks at [[loc_ashwell]] between hauls and recruits there
when he's short.

a haul. "The Reach doesn't care how tough you talk. Neither do I."

Hides in [[loc_sump]] and trades rumours for anything warm. Owes the [[fac_saltline]]
more than she'll ever pay, so she stays where [[npc_vosk]] won't wade. Small,
quick, frightened in a way that's made her very good at knowing things before they
happen. The cheapest reliable source in the Undervault, if you can stand the smell.

**Speech:** fast, hushed, always glancing past you. Prices everything in favours
and protection. Genuinely grateful for kindness, which is rare enough to trust.

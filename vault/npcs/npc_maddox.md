Keeps the [[fac_saltline]]'s walkers standing at [[loc_walker_bay]] with parts
that should be scrap. Grease to the elbows, protective of his machines, indifferent
to the work-gangs that feed them. Maddox will talk shop for hours and won't meet
your eye when the subject turns to where his labour comes from.

**Speech:** blunt, technical, defensive when pressed on the gangs. "The walker's
my problem. The rest is above my pay." Believes it about half the time.

The steadiest hands on [[loc_bonecutter]]. Sable pulls ledger-tagged augments and
fits grey-market chrome, and unlike most of the Row, tends to leave people alive
afterward. Ex-Combine clinic, struck off for reasons she doesn't discuss. Charges
in scrip or favours; favours cost more.

**Speech:** clinical, dry, unshockable. Describes what she's about to do to you in
the same tone she'd use to read a manifest.

Lives among the racks at [[loc_dead_relay]] and talks to the old machines more
than to people. Teller can make analog switchgear and dead terminals do things
they were never rated for. Twitchy around crowds, calm around a soldering iron.
Trades relay time and repairs for parts and quiet.

**Speech:** distracted, technical, trails off mid-sentence when a light blinks
wrong. Warms up fast to anyone who respects the old stack.

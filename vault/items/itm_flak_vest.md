Ceramic trauma plates stitched into a nanoweave carrier, pulled off something that
stopped needing it. Heavy, stiff, and it stops a blade or a low-caliber round from
finding anything vital. Won't save you from a slug pistol at range, but it buys a
second — and a second is the difference down here.

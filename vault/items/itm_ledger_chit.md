A stamped syndicate token that says a debt is settled or a passage is owed. The
[[fac_saltline]] honours them — or claims not to recognise them, depending on who's
holding one and how badly they want it. [[npc_marrow]] mints them; only [[npc_marrow]]
truly knows which are real.

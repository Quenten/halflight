A wire-bound bundle of Grayreach salvage — dead-node ceramic, drive-core shielding,
a handful of components that predate the colony's decline. The [[fac_dredge]] haul
it in; the [[loc_drip_market]] turns it into scrip. Some pieces read faintly warm
on a meter and nobody asks why.

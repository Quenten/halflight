The lowest gallery still called part of the Undervault — a flooded construction
void where the city's runoff collects and does not leave. Knee-deep in cold black
water, ceilings furred with something pale and slow. People who need to not be
found come to the Sump. [[npc_wren]] is one of them, trading rumours for anything
warm.

The water hides holes that go down further than anyone has come back from. Watch
your footing. The Sump keeps what it takes.

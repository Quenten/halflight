Where the sealed Undervault cracks open onto the Grayreach — a breach in the outer
gallery wall, patched and re-patched, where the storm-flats leak in. The [[fac_dredge]]
stage here before a haul, checking seals and saying their goodbyes. Past the Seam
the survey maps just say DO NOT TRANSIT. See [[lore_grayreach]].

The salt wind finds every gap. The temperature drops. Instruments read wrong this
close to the dead terraformer nodes, and the wrongness gets worse the further out
you go.

## Secret

Three crews went out the Seam last cycle chasing the same node-signature. None
came back, and the signature is still transmitting — closer now than it was.

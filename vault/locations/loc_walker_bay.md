A cavern of chain-hoists and half-stripped exoframes — mining walkers retrofitted
with autocannon, the muscle the [[fac_saltline]] fields when a territory dispute
stops being about words. [[npc_maddox]] keeps them running with parts that should
have been condemned a decade ago.

Work-gangs move under the hoists, tags burned off, saying nothing. Some of them
paid the Saltline for passage offworld and ended up here instead. The walkers get
maintained better than the people who build them.

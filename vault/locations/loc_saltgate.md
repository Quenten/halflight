The throat of the Undervault: a blast-door gallery where the Terraces end and the
off-ledger deep begins. The [[fac_saltline]] runs the gate; [[fac_watch_command]]
posts a booth beside it and pretends that means something. [[npc_harrow]] works
the Combine side, checking tags nobody respects.

Air tastes of coolant and crowd. Everyone slows here, because everyone is being
counted — by the charter, and by the syndicate, and the two counts never match.

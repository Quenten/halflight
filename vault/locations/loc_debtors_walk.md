A long service corridor where the [[fac_saltline]] keeps its accounts in person.
Names are chalked on the walls with figures beside them; some names get crossed
out, and not because they paid. [[npc_vosk]] works the Walk, and people who see
him coming find urgent reasons to be elsewhere.

The lighting is deliberate — bright enough to read a face, dim enough to miss a
blade. Owing scrip in Cinderreach is a countdown; the Walk is where it runs out.

Off-ledger reactors bolted into a gallery never rated to hold them, feeding power
the [[fac_saltline]] sells back to the deck at a markup. The air shimmers with
heat and something that makes your fillings ache. [[npc_pell]] nurses the cores
and knows exactly how close to failure each one runs, because keeping that secret
is the only thing keeping her alive.

Dosimeters here are painted over. Nobody wants to read them. Long shifts on the
Row are a slow way to sell your marrow for scrip.

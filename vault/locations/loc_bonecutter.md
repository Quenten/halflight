Unlicensed body-shops behind curtains of surgical plastic. This is where augments
come off — ledger-tag and all — and where cheaper, dirtier ones go on. The
Undervault's real trade: the Combine sells you chrome on credit, and the Row cuts
it back out when you can't pay the licence. [[npc_sable]] runs the cleanest table
on the Row, which is not saying much.

It smells of antiseptic losing a fight with rot. The anaesthetic is rationed.
Screams are normal enough that nobody looks up.

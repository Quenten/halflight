Off-ledger trade under a ceiling that never stops weeping condensate. Stalls of
salvaged chrome, grey-market stims, ration chits, and things with the serial
numbers ground off. If it can be sold without the Combine noticing, it's sold
here. [[npc_quill]] keeps a stall near the sump-drain and hears everything.

Nothing here has a warranty and nothing here is clean. The [[fac_saltline]] takes
a cut of every table; the tables that don't pay don't reopen.

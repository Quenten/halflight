A quantum relay station that stopped talking to the Crest years ago. The dishes
are dark, but the old stack beneath them still hums — analog switchgear, CRT
terminals, pneumatic doors that outlive every upgrade meant to replace them.
[[npc_teller]] lives among the racks and keeps the old machines fed.

When the storms cut power to everything newer, the Dead Relay is what still works.
That makes it useful, and quietly contested.

## Secret

Teller has spliced a hard line into a Combine maintenance channel the Crest forgot
it owned. It still carries traffic. Anyone who could read it would know things the
[[fac_meridian_combine]] would kill to keep buried.

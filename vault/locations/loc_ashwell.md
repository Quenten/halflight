A drinking hole sunk into an old cistern, lit by a ring of failing emergency
strips. Neutral ground by long habit — [[fac_saltline]] muscle and [[fac_dredge]]
salvagers drink at the same bar and settle nothing here, because the one rule of
the Ashwell is that you carry it back outside. [[npc_marrow]] holds a booth in the
back; [[npc_thane]] nurses rotgut between hauls.

The rotgut is cut with reclaimer runoff and tastes like it. People come for the
quiet, not the drink.

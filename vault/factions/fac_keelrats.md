A scavenger syndicate that became the de facto government of everything below the Rings. They control the [[loc_enginecore]] and the [[loc_cargofathoms]], run the black market in salvaged tech and unlicensed augments, and broker illegal power taps off the reactor. Brutal creditors and reliable partners both — in Keelrat territory their word outweighs the Charter.

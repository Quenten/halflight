Descendants of the surviving command crew, claiming legal authority over the Husk through inherited Charter rank. They hold the [[loc_bridgeworks]] and the [[loc_rings_upper]], enforce Charter Law through their armed arm the Wardens, and gatekeep [[loc_vaultwell]] access permits — the single most valuable thing anyone on the ship controls. Rivals with the [[fac_keelrats]] and the [[fac_wakeful]]; they tolerate the [[fac_communion]] for keeping the lower decks calm.

The charter corporation. Owns the air ration, the water, the orbital yards, and
the ledger every colonist is born into. Not cruel by policy — cruel by accounting.
The Combine rules from the Crest and rarely descends; the Undervault is a line
item it has already written off. See [[lore_scrip]].

Its interest down here is narrow: augment ledger-tags, unpaid air debt, and
anything the [[fac_saltline]] salvages that the Combine would rather stayed lost.

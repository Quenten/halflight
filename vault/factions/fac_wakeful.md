Small, growing, and branded dangerous radicals by the Directorate. They believe the current order is a slow-motion mass killing by inaction, and push — sometimes violently — to thaw sleepers regardless of the cost to the living. The [[fac_directorate]] wants them gone; the [[fac_keelrats]] fear every waked sleeper as one more mouth against the underdeck economy.

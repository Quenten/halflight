Combine security. Underpaid, negotiable, until you make the Watchlist — then it
stops being negotiable. They post a token presence at [[loc_saltgate]] to remind
the Undervault the charter still exists, and pull back before the reminder costs
them anyone. [[npc_harrow]] holds the post here, and hates it.

Watch Command does not patrol deep. Below the market the lights belong to the
[[fac_saltline]], and everyone knows the arithmetic.

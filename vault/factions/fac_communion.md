A religious order that venerates [[lore_steward|STEWARD]] as a wounded shepherd rather than a broken machine — they hold that the [[lore_the_long_fall|Long Fall]] was a sacrifice, not a failure. They keep shrines at old terminal rooms across the ship, strongest around the [[loc_vaultwell]], which they treat as sacred ground. Genuinely charitable in places; often the only ones caring for the ship's forgotten. They spar with the [[fac_directorate]] over who really controls STEWARD, on doctrine rather than by force.

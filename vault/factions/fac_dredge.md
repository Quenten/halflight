Independent salvage crews who work the [[loc_the_seam]] and the Grayreach beyond
it — the storm-flats, the dead terraformer nodes, the zones the survey maps mark
DO NOT TRANSIT. They bring back what the [[fac_saltline]] wants and resent the
cut the syndicate takes for letting them pass. Loose, quarrelsome, loyal only to
the crew you shipped out with. See [[lore_grayreach]].

[[npc_thane]] runs a Dredge crew and drinks at [[loc_ashwell]] between hauls.

The dominant syndicate below the Terraces. Off-ledger salvage, chrome without
tags, debt, and quiet passage offworld. In the Undervault their word outranks the
charter. Brutal creditors, reliable partners — the difference is whether you owe
them. See [[lore_saltline]].

They keep the [[loc_saltgate]] open because a gate they control is worth more than
a wall. [[npc_marrow]] speaks for them this deck; [[npc_vosk]] collects what
[[npc_marrow]] can't ask for politely.

## Secret

The Saltline's "quiet passage offworld" is a short list and a shorter drop. Most
who pay for a berth are sold to the [[loc_walker_bay]] work-gangs instead. Only
[[npc_marrow]] and the yard bosses know which berths are real.

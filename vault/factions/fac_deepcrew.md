An explorer's guild that maps the [[loc_deephull]] — the collapsed, unmapped aft sections of the ship. They sell maps and salvage, stay roughly neutral, and run thin on numbers but heavy on reputation. Missing members are common and rarely discussed. The [[fac_keelrats]] buy most of what they bring back.
